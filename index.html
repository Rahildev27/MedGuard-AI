About MediGuard AI

MediGuard AI is an AI-assisted medicine verification prototype designed to help users identify potentially suspicious medicine packages through visual layout checks, manufacturer directory lookup, and batch formatting rules.

Medical Safety Disclaimer

MediGuard provides an AI-assisted verification assessment and does not guarantee medicine authenticity. Always verify suspicious medicines with an authorized pharmacist, manufacturer, or relevant regulatory authority.
